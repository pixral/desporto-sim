# Playtest checklist

Start with `.\start.ps1` (or the Browser pane while a Claude session is running). Each item is a few minutes.

## 1. A fresh company (Normal)
- [ ] **New company** → Normal, any CEO style, a new seed → *Open the doors*.
- [ ] Press **Run** at 1×. Morning: people gather at The Bench; 11:00: tipsters walk to their desks and type; 16:00:
      watching; 23:30: the office darkens, desks glow, floating +€/−€ numbers appear.
- [ ] Click a tipster: check every tab (Overview, Bets, Performance, Relationships, History, Decisions).
      In **Decisions**, open an AI call and read the prompt the agent saw.
- [ ] Click a desk room: the department panel opens.

## 2. Management and drama (16× or 64×)
- [ ] On the 1st of each month: the CEO stands in the meeting room with a memo bubble; check the **CEO** view for
      what was done and what was rejected.
- [ ] Watch for speech bubbles: hires, firings, promotions, "Under review…", arguments (red zig-zag), poaching,
      raise demands, refusals of LAB strategies.
- [ ] **LAB** view: experiments with in-sample and holdout results; deployed strategies.
- [ ] Keep going to **1 June**: the *Awards night* screen should appear once, and stay clickable in **History**.

## 3. Money
- [ ] **Money** view: value/cash/bankroll chart (hover for values, *Table* toggle), monthly results, desks.
- [ ] Do the KPI numbers at the top agree with the Money view?

## 4. Saves
- [ ] **Save**, run a few days, then **Saves** → *Load* the earlier save: the date and numbers go back exactly.
- [ ] Close the app and start it again: it resumes the latest unfinished company.

## 5. Endings and difficulty
- [ ] A **Hard** company with an aggressive or chaotic CEO at *max* speed usually goes bankrupt: check the end
      screen and the history.
- [ ] An **Easy** company should usually grow.

## What to report
For anything odd: what you did, the in-game date, and a screenshot. The **History** and **AI log** views usually
show why something happened.
